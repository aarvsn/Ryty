# Authors

## Ryty

Ryty is a fork of [AnyPS5](https://github.com/boykopovar/AnyPS5) by
Andrii Semkiv (boykopovar) and its contributors; the relinker architecture,
the PRX library implementations and the shader recompiler originate from that
project. Ryty is maintained under the same GPL-2.0 license with gratitude to
the original authors.

Ryty-specific work (instruction lowering for the SHA extension family and
AMD-only instructions, the porting report, the Qt6 GUI, CI and the packaging
pipeline) is contributed by the Ryty maintainers and contributors.
